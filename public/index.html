Cookie dough for this bake

1 batch = 12 balls (usually 13, count as 12). Dough is made in whole batches. The part used for brookies comes out of it, and all the rest becomes frozen Cookie Dough Balls. Leave blank to make just enough for this bake. Example: 1 brookie uses 0.5 batch, so making 2 batches freezes 18 balls. To make dough only, set the item qty to 0 and set batches below.
