Batch Planning

Shopify orders

Pulls orders from Shopify every hour and turns them into bake plans, dated on the pickup or delivery date from the order. A box of cookies uses frozen balls first, and only makes new dough for the shortfall. Orders with no date, or a product with no mapping, are flagged below, never planned on the order date. Dismiss a flag once it has been dealt with and it will not come back on the next sync. Dismissed flags are listed below and can be restored.

Shopping List — Weekly Restock

Flagged weekly: anything below minimum, plus what planned bakes will need. Buy enough to cover planned bakes and get back to the minimum. Items flagged "short before the next shop" will run short before Friday, so buy them sooner if you can. Maurice buys once a week on Friday. Enter how much you bought in any unit and click Add to inventory to add it to stock straight away. Cookie Dough Balls are not on this list, they are made in Batch Planning, not bought.

Stock History

Every stock change, newest first. Undo puts the stock back, or un-completes a bake plan and puts its ingredients back.
