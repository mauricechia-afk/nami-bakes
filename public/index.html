Inventory Management

Tap a quantity to adjust it. All quantities are editable.

Stock bar: the bar is full at 3 times the minimum, a comfortable level. Teal means healthy, gold means getting low, red means below minimum. The label under each bar shows how many times the minimum you have.

Recipe Management

Add recipe (per portion)

Pick each ingredient from your Inventory. If an ingredient is not listed, add it to Inventory first, then come back here.

Batch Planning

Plan a bake

Plan finished items — fractions allowed (e.g. 1.5). The app breaks them into base sub-recipes and shows each one scaled, for baking in one go.

Cookie dough for this bake

1 batch = 12 balls (usually 13, count as 12). Dough is made in whole batches. The part used for brookies comes out of it, and all the rest becomes frozen Cookie Dough Balls. Leave blank to make just enough for this bake. Example: 1 brookie uses 0.5 batch, so making 2 batches freezes 18 balls. To make dough only, set the item qty to 0 and set batches below.

Saved plans

Shopping List — Weekly Restock

Flagged weekly: anything below minimum, plus what planned bakes will need. Buy enough to cover planned bakes and get back to the minimum. Items flagged "short before the next shop" will run short before Friday, so buy them sooner if you can. Maurice buys once a week on Friday. Tick an item you bought, enter how much you bought in any unit, and confirm to add it to inventory straight away. Cookie Dough Balls are not on this list, they are made in Batch Planning, not bought.

Stock History

Every stock change, newest first. Undo puts the stock back, or un-completes a bake plan and puts its ingredients back.

Backup

Download a full copy of your stock, recipes, plans and history. If anything ever goes wrong, restore it from the file.
